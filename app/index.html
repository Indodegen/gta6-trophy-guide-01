    Road to the Platinum

        Missable trophies — 25 to guard

        No trophy is confirmed missable by Rockstar. These 25 are flagged because the moment behind them cannot be replayed: story missions cannot be replayed, and a one-time setup cannot be rebuilt. Keep a manual save before every big mission — the game gives you 15 manual save slots. Sources: Exophase, PSNProfiles, PowerPyx, Push Square.
