    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Five stages, built from the official descriptions: play the story first, close the stranger strands, grind the activities, sweep the collectibles, then clean up the chaos and the money. The platinum pops when the other 54 are done — there is no separate task for it.

    Missable trophies — read this before the finale

    A trophy is flagged missable when the official description ties it to a single story moment, a partner who is only available for a while, a one-time choice, or a one-off event. Story missions in GTA are normally replayable, so a flag means at risk if the moment passes, not certainly lost: if you miss one, try the mission replay menu first, and only then consider a new playthrough. Always keep a manual save from before the final mission.

    Settings & save data

    Spoiler setting

    When spoiler-safe mode is on, story trophies you have not earned yet show as “Hidden story trophy” — their names and official descriptions stay hidden until you tick them off. It is on by default for new visitors who have not earned anything yet.

    Export & import

    Your checklist lives in this browser (localStorage) and never leaves it unless you export it. Export downloads a JSON file with your earned trophies and settings; import reads such a file back.

    Reset

    Clear every tick and setting stored in this browser. Export first if you want a copy.
